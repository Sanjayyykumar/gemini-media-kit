# Example prompts

## Images
- Children's colouring page: `gmk image "colouring-book line art of Lord Krishna playing flute under a tree, thick clean outlines, no shading, white background" --aspect 3:4`
- Game asset: `gmk image "glowing emerald gem icon, flat vector, transparent-looking plain background, centered" --aspect 1:1`
- Product mockup: `gmk image "paperback book standing on a wooden desk, soft window light" --input cover.png`

## Video
- Ambient loop: `gmk video "gentle rain on jungle leaves, close-up, soft thunder in the distance" --seconds 8`
- Character shot: `gmk video "the girl lifts the gem and it glows brighter, camera slowly pushes in" --image girl.png`
- Vertical reel: `gmk video "fast-paced montage feel: temple doors open, light floods out, whoosh sound" --aspect 9:16`

## Text
- `gmk text "Give 5 YouTube titles for a Mahabharat kids story video" --think low`
- `gmk text "What's in this image? Suggest a better prompt to recreate it" --input ref.png`
